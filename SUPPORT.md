## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

### 1. How do I switch citation styles?
Use the **Style** dropdown inside the floating control dock on Google Scholar (or in the extension popup) to switch instantly between **APA 7th, MLA 9th, Chicago 17th, IEEE, Harvard, and BibTeX**.

### 2. How does 0% CAPTCHA Volume, Issue, Page & Abstract Enrichment work?
When you click **"⚡ Enrich Page"** or **"⚡ Enrich"**, ScholarCite Express connects directly to open, free academic APIs (**Crossref, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar**) via an extension background service worker. No automated requests are ever sent to Google Scholar, ensuring **0% robot warnings or CAPTCHAs**.

### 3. How do I export my research items to AI models or reference managers?
Click **"📥 Export Page Dataset"** on the floating control dock or use the extension popup to export items into:
- **Markdown (.md)**: Best for Gemini, NotebookLM, ChatGPT, and Claude. Includes pre-built Literature Review prompt presets.
- **JSON (.json)**: Best for RAG applications and database ingestion.
- **Word (.docx)**: Clean Academic References or Annotated Bibliographies with full abstracts.
- **BibTeX (.bib) & RIS (.ris)**: Compatible with Zotero, Mendeley, and EndNote.
- **Open Access PDF Links (.txt)**: Clean list of direct PDF URLs for downloader tools.
